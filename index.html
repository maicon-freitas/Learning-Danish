Method

Layered study roadmap

1

Recognize

Read Danish to English, then English to Danish.

Goal: recognize words without translating everything.
2

Speak

Read phrases out loud and repeat them without looking.

Goal: produce basic sentences.
3

Substitute

Swap names, places, foods and verbs inside the same pattern.

Goal: create new sentences.
4

Survive

Use real expressions for school, shopping, transport, family and the city.

Goal: get by in real situations.
5

Automate

Review short phrases every day until you answer without thinking in another language.

Goal: speak faster.

Your 60 minutes

One hour a day, one small step at a time.

Follow the same six blocks every day. You do not need to learn everything at once: the weekly focus tells you what to use in the practice blocks.

10 min

Review

Flip 10–15 old flashcards. Say the Danish answer before you turn each card.

10 min

Learn

Read one module card. Choose only 5 new words or 2 useful phrases.

10 min

Verbs

Practise one verb in the present and past. Make three short sentences with it.

10 min

Questions

Choose one question word, ask three questions, then answer them out loud.

10 min

Build and speak

Use the sentence builder to create five sentences about your own life. Listen and repeat.

10 min

Check and save

Do two quiz questions. Write down one mistake and one sentence you want to remember.
